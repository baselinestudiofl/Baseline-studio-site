Live Music for Corporate Events Miami
people stay in the room.

A corporate crowd doesn't show up ready the way a wedding crowd does. People come straight from work, they know maybe a third of the room, and the first half hour decides whether the night opens up or stays polite. Live music is the fastest way to move that. A trio playing while people find their table does more for a room than anything you can put on a screen.

Request a Proposal Full Corporate Production

Live music at a corporate event has a different job than it does at a wedding. Nobody is here for a first dance. What you're managing is a room of people who mostly don't know each other, an agenda that has to stay on time, and a window at the end where you either get a real party or everyone leaves after dessert.

We book and run the music around that. A jazz trio through cocktail hour and registration. A string quartet or a solo pianist while dinner goes out and the program runs. A Latin band or a party band once the awards are done and the room is ready to move. The setup people ask for most is a saxophonist or percussionist playing live over a DJ set, because it gives you the feel of a band while the DJ keeps hold of the tempo.

The music runs through our system, not the venue's. Every musician plays through the same PA carrying your presenters, so there's no point in the night where someone is re-patching cables while your VP waits at the podium. We hold the run of show, we sound check before doors, and the handoff into the DJ set happens on cue.

Size is a real decision. A 60-person leadership dinner doesn't need an eight-piece band, and a 600-person gala will swallow a solo guitarist. We scale the group to the room and the headcount, and we'll tell you when a smaller setup will serve you better.

Live music is one piece of a corporate night. If you're also weighing LED walls, stage lighting, or branded uplighting, the full corporate production page covers how it all fits together under one crew.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does live music for a corporate event in Miami cost?

Live music for a Miami corporate event generally runs from around $900 for a solo pianist or guitarist to $6,000 and up for a full band, depending on the number of musicians, how long they play, and whether they're covering cocktail hour, the dinner program, or the after-party. Send us your date, venue, and headcount and we'll put a number to it.

Can you provide a live band for a corporate event, or only solo musicians?

Both. We book from a roster that runs from a solo pianist up through a jazz trio, a string quartet, and a full band for the after-party portion of the night. The right size depends on the room and the headcount. A 60-person leadership dinner doesn't need an eight-piece band, and a 600-person gala will swallow a solo guitarist.

Live band or DJ for a corporate event?

Most corporate nights want both, and they do different jobs. Live music carries cocktail hour and dinner, where you want atmosphere without a dance floor. A DJ carries the back half, where you need to control tempo and read a room that's finally loosening up. The version people ask for most is a saxophonist or percussionist playing live over the DJ set, which gives you the feel of a band while the DJ keeps hold of the pacing.

Do the musicians play through your sound system or the venue's?

Ours. Every musician plays through the same PA carrying your presenters, so there's no point in the night where someone is re-patching cables while a speaker waits at the podium. We hold the run of show, we sound check before doors, and the handoff into the DJ set happens on cue.

How far in advance should we book live music for a corporate event in Miami?

For a gala or an annual event on a weekend date in season, 3 to 6 months gives you the best choice of musicians. Weekday corporate events and off-peak dates can often be put together in 4 to 6 weeks. If your date is already set, reach out and we'll tell you honestly what's still available.
